2. PRODUCT CLASSIFICATIONS & CAPABILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Marso specializes in 8 primary product classifications:

[Category 1] Reclaimed and Crumb Rubber (Reclem Rubber / Generato)
  - Granules sizes: (1-2) mm, (1-4) mm, fine rubber powder.
  - FIFA-certified crumb rubber for artificial turf, running tracks, asphalt rubberized roads, and sports flooring.

[Category 2] Rubber Tile Flooring
  - High-density sound and shock-absorbing tiles for gyms, fitness centers, nurseries, playgrounds, tartan tracks, bulletproof shooting range walls, and swimming pool surroundings.
  - Standard sizes include 50x50 cm, 100x100 cm, 120x120 cm with thickness from 10mm to 50mm.

[Category 3] Rubber Mat Flooring
  - Heavy-duty anti-bacterial mats for cattle/cow farm flooring, horse stables, agricultural applications, and animal welfare.

[Category 4] Industrial Rubber Flooring
  - Fire-retardant sheets, electrical-insulating switchboard mats (up to 50kV), anti-vibration machine damper pads, and garage floor armor.

[Category 5] Rubber Automotive Spare Parts
  - Engine mounts, suspension bushings, gaskets, seals, heavy bumpers, marine/port ship fenders, and custom industrial auto rubber components.

[Category 6] Rubber Car Mats
  - Custom all-weather floor liners and tailored car mats designed for extreme wear resistance.

[Category 7] Constructive Rubber Industries
  - Structural elastomeric bridge bearing pads, expansion joints, generator shock mounts, EPDM facade rollers, neoprene construction grades, and building isolation dampers.

[Category 8] Reverse Engineering & Custom Fabrication
  - Full custom manufacturing capability: Marso can replicate, design, and produce any custom rubber shape, profile, gasket, or molded part from technical CAD drawings or physical customer samples.

3. RUBBER COMPOUND & TECHNICAL ENGINEERING SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Rubber Polymer Types Used:
  * EPDM (Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer): Exceptional UV, ozone, weather, and heat resistance (-40°C to +150°C).
  * NBR / Nitrile: High resistance to petroleum oils, fuels, grease, and hydraulic fluids.
  * SBR (Styrene-Butadiene Rubber): Excellent abrasion resistance, high tensile strength, used in tiles, crumb, and general mats.
  * Neoprene (Chloroprene / CR): Balanced resistance to oils, flame, weather, and mechanical stress.
  * Silicone Rubber: Ultra-high temperature tolerance (-60°C to +250°C) for food-grade, medical, and extreme heat seals.
  * Viton (FKM): Extreme chemical, acid, solvent, and fuel resistance up to +250°C.
  * Natural Rubber (NR): Superior elasticity, tear strength, and resilience.

- Physical Property Ranges:
  * Hardness Range: 40 to 90 Shore A (customizable per spec).
  * Tensile Strength: 3.5 N/mm² to over 20 N/mm².
  * Elongation at Break: 150% to over 500%.
  * Abrasion & Corrosion Loss: High resistance per ISO/ASTM standards.
